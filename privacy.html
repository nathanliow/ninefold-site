Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Ninefold does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server.

Game messages

When you start a game or finish one, Ninefold puts a message in your conversation containing the cards, the target, and each player's move count. You choose whether to send it. Messages are sent by Apple's Messages app under Apple's privacy policy; Ninefold never sees your conversations, contacts, or names. Player names in game bubbles are filled in by Messages on each device.

On your device

Ninefold saves your finished scores and your card display setting on your device so a game can't be replayed. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Ninefold collects no information from anyone, including children.
