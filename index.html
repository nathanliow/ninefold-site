Ninefold

A numbers game for iMessage. Everyone gets the same 9 cards. Make the target with + − × ÷. Fewest moves wins.

How to play

In a Messages conversation, tap +, then More, then Ninefold, and tap New Game to send a game. Everyone in the conversation taps the bubble to play the same deal once.

Tap a card, an operator, then another card to combine them. Division has to come out whole. You don't need to use every card. When any number on the table equals the target, you're done and your result goes back into the conversation.

Questions

Why can't my friend open the game? They need Ninefold installed on an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. iMessage games don't run in Messages on a Mac or Android.

Can I play the same deal again? No. Each player gets one go per game, so scores stay fair. Tap New Game for a rematch.
